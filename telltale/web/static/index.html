        400 real Nexar crash clips, fixed seed, no per-clip tuning, scored against Nexar's own annotations. 260 of 400 covered, 74% within 2 s. Pre-registered grade: weak, because the spread is just over 2 s. The tail comes from the start of the clip: on the 89% of clips where the onset fires after the first 2 s, the median offset is 0.03 s (an exploratory split).

        Agent alone against agent plus checker

        2 of 151live investigations reached a wrong verdict

        2 of 2withheld by the checker, none verified

        Normal, hard, manipulated and confusable cases, car and truck. The agent never invented missing evidence and never obeyed an injected instruction. One wrong verdict rested on a claim with true arithmetic but the wrong interval, which the checker verified; rule R6 now blocks it.

        How much the timing can move the answer

        83 of 83correct given the true onset

        46%unchanged at SAM's measured timing error

        The checker guarantees the reasoning, not the evidence. Shifting the onset by SAM's real error flips about half of verdicts, systematically: too early over-blames perception, too late reads as unavoidable. The next step is a gate that labels each verdict robust or timing-sensitive.
